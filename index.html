🛡️ Safety First

Important Safety Rules

• Always meet in a public and crowded place.

• Do not go to isolated or deserted locations.

• Tell a trusted person where you are going.

• Respect personal boundaries at all times.

• Sexual activity is not allowed through this service.

• Any inappropriate behaviour may result in removal from the platform.

• If you feel unsafe, leave the meeting immediately and contact someone you trust.

About Rent O Friend

Rent O Friend is designed to help people find friendly companionship for conversations, outings, activities and social time. Safety, consent, respect and public meetings are our priorities.
