Safety Comes First

Always use common sense when meeting someone new.

Important Safety Rules

  • Always meet for the first time in a busy public place.
  • Do not go to isolated or deserted places with someone you just met.
  • Tell a trusted person where you are going.
  • Do not share passwords, OTPs, banking details or sensitive information.
  • Respect personal boundaries at all times.
  • Rent O Friend is for social companionship and activities — not sexual activity.
  • If someone makes you uncomfortable, leave the meeting and report the profile.
